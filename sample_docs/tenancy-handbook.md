# Tenancy Handbook

## Starting a lease

All new tenants complete a reference check before keys are issued. The standard
security deposit is one month of rent, payable in full before the tenancy start
date. Annual leases require the deposit plus the first month of rent up front.

## Ending a lease

Tenants must give thirty days written notice before ending a lease. Notice given
partway through a month runs from the first day of the following month. Leaving
without notice forfeits the deposit.

## Deposits

Deposits are returned within fourteen days of a satisfactory final inspection.
Deductions are itemised in writing and cover unpaid rent, damage beyond fair wear
and tear, and professional cleaning where the property is left unclean.

## Pets

Pets are permitted in ground floor units only, and only with written consent
obtained before the animal moves in. A pet deposit of two hundred pounds applies
per animal and is held separately from the security deposit.

## Alterations

Tenants may hang pictures using removable fixings. Painting, drilling, and any
change to fitted units require written permission from the property manager.
