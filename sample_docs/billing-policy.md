# Billing Policy

## Payment schedule

Rent is collected by direct debit on the first working day of each month.
Failed collections are retried once after five working days.

## Late payment

A late payment fee applies once rent is more than fourteen days overdue. The fee
is three percent of the outstanding balance, charged once per late period rather
than per day.

## Refunds

Refunds on annual plans are issued on a pro rata basis for the unused months.
Refunds are processed within ten working days of the cancellation being
confirmed, and are returned to the original payment method.

## Service charges

Service charges are reviewed each April and any change is communicated at least
sixty days before it takes effect. The charge covers communal cleaning, grounds
maintenance, lighting in shared areas, and buildings insurance.
