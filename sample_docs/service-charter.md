# Service Charter

## Response times

Support requests submitted through the tenant portal are answered within one
business day. Requests received at weekends are answered on the next working day.

## Repairs

Emergency repairs are attended to within four hours of being reported. An
emergency means a loss of heating in winter, a total loss of power or water, a
blocked sole toilet, or anything that makes the property unsafe.

Urgent repairs are attended to within two working days. Routine maintenance is
scheduled within one week of the request being logged.

## Escalation

If a repair is not resolved within the stated window, tenants may escalate to the
regional manager, who responds within two working days with a revised plan.

## Inspections

Routine property inspections take place twice a year. Tenants receive at least
twenty four hours notice before any visit.
